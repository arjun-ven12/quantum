# Educational quality audit

Audited: 107 authored concepts and 8 source workbook problems. This is a content and feature audit, not a claim of external pedagogical validation or proof of every equation.

## What the current lesson flow actually provides

- Every authored concept has an intuition, mathematical statement, notation list, formula, worked case, checked derivation choices, concept question, numerical check, source page, prerequisite/related links and a live lab prompt. The worked case is visible in the lesson, and the guided mode reveals the concepts in a teaching sequence.
- Chapter 1 displays an additional authored mental model, relevance explanation and alternate worked view for each of its 15 concepts, plus answer-specific explanations for its concept-check distractors and common numeric mistakes.
- Page 38 now has 8 source problems and 8 supplementary transfer cases, each with a derivation decision and checked numerical answer.
- Review intervals derive from local answer, error, and hint events; rapid same-session successes do not count as spaced recalls. The rule is transparent and is not a validated mastery model.

## Remaining weaknesses

- Chapters 2–9 still rely on concise concept overlays and generic error feedback for many wrong answers. Their guided mode sequences existing material but is not a substitute for individually authored alternate explanations and misconception maps.
- Numerical practice accepts arithmetic expressions and fractions; it cannot reliably grade an open symbolic derivation or a free-text scientific argument. Derivation steps are selected responses.
- Most concepts have one fixed numerical case; later chapters need more parameterised transfer problems and independent mathematical review of each new case.
- Some abstract source diagrams still use the original PDF and explanatory text rather than a dedicated manipulable visual.
- Review scheduling uses a transparent heuristic; evidence from this app has not validated that it predicts long-term mastery.
- Ideal small-system simulation does not represent noise, hardware scale, or a practical speedup. Optional online AI tutoring remains separate and unimplemented.
