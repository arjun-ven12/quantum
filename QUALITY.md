# Educational quality audit

Audited: 107 authored concepts and 8 source workbook problems. This is a content and feature audit, not a claim of external pedagogical validation or proof of every equation.

## What the current lesson flow actually provides

- Every authored concept has an intuition, mathematical statement, notation list, formula, worked case, checked derivation choices, concept question, numerical check, source page, prerequisite/related links and a live lab prompt.
- All 107 concepts now have an individually authored supplementary mental model and alternative worked view. The 92 concepts in Chapters 2–9 have revised checks with plausible wrong answers and feedback that identifies the specific misconception.
- 34 later concepts also diagnose a common wrong numerical answer; other wrong numbers receive a problem-specific hint and worked method.
- Guided derivation attempts now reveal a hint before the solution; finishing one displays a complete reasoning recap. Repeated taps after a correct answer do not add extra mastery events.
- Numeric answers accept fractions, powers, roots and common functions using a deterministic offline parser.
- Page 38 has 8 source problems and 8 supplementary transfer cases, each with a checked numerical answer.
- Review intervals derive from local answer, error, and hint events; rapid same-session successes do not count as spaced recalls. The rule is transparent and is not a validated mastery model.

## Remaining weaknesses

- The alternate explanations are individually authored but have not been reviewed by an independent quantum computing educator. Numerical examples that state new claims need continued reference checks.
- Most concepts still have one fixed numerical case and two fixed derivation choices. More parameterised transfer problems and open symbolic work would better test independent understanding.
- The deterministic answer checker handles arithmetic, powers and common functions but does not grade open symbolic derivations or free-text scientific arguments.
- Some abstract source diagrams remain in the PDF and explanatory text rather than a dedicated manipulable visual.
- Review scheduling uses a transparent heuristic; evidence from this app has not validated that it predicts long-term mastery.
- Ideal small-system simulation does not represent hardware scale or a practical speedup. Optional online AI tutoring remains separate and unimplemented.
